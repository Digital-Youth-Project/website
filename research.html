Our work

Research

Our work sits at the intersection of developmental psychology, digital media, and mental health. We ask how problematic patterns of social media use and gaming develop, who is most at risk, and how digital media use relates to young people's mental health and well-being. To answer these questions, we use a range of methods: qualitative interviews, longitudinal cohort data, laboratory tasks, and intensive smartphone-based tracking. In our research, we try to build a nuanced picture and contribute to young people's development and well-being in a digital world.
