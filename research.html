Our work

Research

Our work sits at the intersection of developmental psychology, digital media, and mental health. We focus on how problematic patterns of social media and gaming develop and who is at risk. To answer these questions we use a range of methods: qualitative interviews, longitudinal cohort data, laboratory tasks, and intensive smartphone-based tracking. In our research, we try to build a nuanced picture and contribute to youths' development and well-being in a digital world.
