News

Recent activity, announcements, and milestones from the Digital Youth Project.

July 2026 Publication

Focus group study published in Journal of Adolescence

Our focus group study with 78 Dutch adolescents is now published, open access, in the Journal of Adolescence. Teenagers described both the benefits and the costs of their digital media use, and called for solutions that go beyond individual self-control, involving parents, peers, schools, and platforms. Read the article →

June 2026 Conference

Focus group findings presented at ISSBD 2026 in Incheon, South Korea

We presented our focus group study in a symposium at the 28th Biennial Meeting of the International Society for the Study of Behavioural Development (ISSBD) in Incheon, South Korea. The meeting is one of the largest international conferences on human development.

June 2026 Conference

Social media content study presented at the VNOP Conference in Rotterdam, the Netherlands

We presented our experimental study on sexualised social media content and pornography-related desire in a symposium at the 2026 conference of the Dutch Society for Developmental Psychology (VNOP) in Rotterdam. This year's theme was how developmental science can respond to societal questions without losing its scientific foundation.

May 2026 Conference

Preliminary findings on social media content presented at APS 2026 in Barcelona, Spain

We presented preliminary findings from our experimental study on sexualised social media content and pornography-related desire in a poster at the 2026 APS Annual Convention in Barcelona. It was the first time the Association for Psychological Science held its annual convention outside the United States.

May 2026 Recruitment

Participants wanted for our smartphone study

We are recruiting young people aged 16 to 21 for a two-week smartphone study on social media use and emotional well-being, with brief surveys of about a minute each. Schools, clinics, and youth organisations that would like to help with recruitment are warmly invited to get in touch.

April 2026 Conference

Early focus group findings presented at SRA 2026 in Toronto, Canada

Luka Todorović presented a poster with early findings from our focus group study at the biennial meeting of the Society for Research on Adolescence (SRA) in Toronto, one of the leading international gatherings of adolescence researchers. Helle Larsen attended the meeting and organised a symposium.

April 2026 Grant

Amsterdam University Fund supports our smartphone study

The Digital Youth Project received a €10,000 grant from the Amsterdam University Fund. The grant makes our two-week smartphone study on daily social media use and emotional well-being possible, and we are grateful for the support.

January 2026 Publication

ADHD study published in Addictive Behaviors

Our laboratory study on ADHD symptoms and problematic digital media use in emerging adults is now published in Addictive Behaviors. The study tested whether inhibitory control, reward sensitivity, and time perception explain why ADHD symptoms are linked to problematic social media use and gaming. Read the article →

September 2025 Publication

Longitudinal study published in Journal of Research on Adolescence

Our two-year longitudinal study of 645 Dutch adolescents is now available open access in the Journal of Research on Adolescence. It addresses one of the field's central questions, whether emotional problems or problematic use come first, and shows that the answer differs between girls and boys. Read the article →

July 2025 Conference

Poster and flash talk at the Digital Media and Developing Minds Congress in Washington, DC, USA

We presented findings on mental health and problematic digital media use in adolescents at this international scientific congress in Washington, DC. The congress brings together leading researchers working at the intersection of technology and youth development.
